The experience

Three hours aboard Stardom, a 141ft tri-deck superyacht, with a live DJ, a BBQ and live pasta station and a stop for a swim. Choose the brunch, sundowner or nightfall sailing.

Stardom is one of the largest yachts offering shared sailings in Dubai. Three decks give you plenty of room to move between the sun deck, the lounge areas and the bar, while the resident DJ sets the mood. The brunch sailing is the sunniest, the sundowner catches golden hour over Palm Jumeirah, and the nightfall sailing shows the skyline lit up.
