The experience

Our most exclusive shared experience: three hours aboard Encore, a 131ft Sunseeker superyacht, with a live sushi station, a DJ and saxophonist and premium drinks along the coastline.

Encore is a 131ft Sunseeker with a sky lounge, a large sun deck and refined interiors. This is the most premium shared experience we offer in Dubai: a limited number of guests, a sushi chef on board, a DJ with a live saxophonist and premium drinks, for a three-hour cruise along the coast.
