Sunset and dinner cruise

⚲ Dubai Marina, Dubai · Shared yacht, by the seat

The experience

A 125ft event yacht with a red-carpet welcome, an international buffet, a live cooking station and live music. Take the sunset sailing for golden-hour views, or the evening sailing for the city lights.

An evening cruise along Dubai Marina aboard a 125ft event yacht. You are welcomed on a red carpet, then enjoy an international buffet with a live cooking station and live music as the skyline lights up. The sunset sailing leaves around 5 pm, the dinner sailing around 8:30 pm.
