The experience

New: one hour from Dubai Harbour along JBR, Ain Dubai and Dubai Marina on a 92ft yacht, with audio sightseeing commentary. Boarding is right beside the Dubai Harbour Yacht Club gate, so the walk is short.

A short, easy outing from Dubai Harbour on a 92ft yacht: one hour along JBR, Ain Dubai and Dubai Marina, with an audio guide. The boarding point is beside the Dubai Harbour Yacht Club gate, so arrival is quick and you spend more time on board. A good option when you have little time or are travelling with children.
